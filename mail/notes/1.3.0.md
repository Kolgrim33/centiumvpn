- **Works in more places.** Some internet providers and countries block Tor. If Centium can't connect the usual way, it now tries other routes by itself until one works, and remembers it for next time.
- **Nothing to set up.** Just tap Connect like before. In places where Tor is blocked, the first connection can take a minute or two while Centium finds a way through.
- **More choice, if you want it.** Under Settings you can now choose a route yourself, or add your own bridges if someone has given you some.
